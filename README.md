# Pomodoro Study Tracker

A study timer built on the Pomodoro technique. Focus in timed sessions, then see how your study time adds up by day, by subject, and across the year.

## Screenshot

![Pomodoro Study Tracker screenshot](./screenshot.png)

## Features

### Focus Timer
- **Drag to set:** Turn the circular dial to pick your session length.
- **Quick adjust:** Fine-tune the time with the +5 and −5 buttons.
- **Presets:** Jump straight to 15, 25, 45, or 60 minutes.
- **Full control:** Start, pause, and reset at any point.

### Subject Tagging
- Label each session with a subject like Math, React, or SQL.
- Subject is optional, so you can start a session without typing anything.

### Progress Tracking
- **Today's total:** The header shows how many minutes you've studied today.
- **Daily routine:** See each day's total study time and number of sessions.
- **Yearly activity heatmap:** A GitHub-style grid of your study days over the past year. Darker squares mean more active days.
- **Recent sessions:** Scroll through your latest sessions with subject, start and end times, and duration.
